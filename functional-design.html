01 · Product model

One network, clear objects.

The public surface is organized around canonical organizations, open-source components, proposals, and campaigns. Budgets remain inside organizations; settlement notes deliberately contain no payee.

bloei.me network

One public catalogue, identity layer, policy set, search surface, and audit history per deployment.

Organizations

Verified legal identities with members, representatives, roles, and one public history.

Own budgets + pledges

Components

Canonical open-source software and building blocks with hierarchy, sources, releases, and stewardship.

Receive proposals + campaigns

Campaigns

Versioned scope, acceptance criteria, supplier team, maximum price, dates, and formal component targets.

Collect commitments

Budgets · private

Organization-owned funding envelopes with cost center, expiry, capacity, and optional component scope.

Proposals · public

Curated demand before a supplier, delivery plan, and price exist. Verified organizations endorse once.

Settlement notes

After acceptance: what each pledger contributes, final price, and completion marked by the lead supplier.

Programs may later curate these canonical objects for discovery. They do not create another identity, budget, policy, or security boundary.

03 · Authority

Organizations act through people.

Roles are narrow and scoped. Completed actions belong to the organization and survive personnel changes; every new action rechecks current membership, authority, budget access, and conflicts.

05 · Funding logic

Fair shares within firm caps.

Each organization publicly promises a maximum percentage and privately reserves its full monetary exposure. The projected split is max-min fair: equal where possible, while never exceeding a cap.

Try the final price

The campaign maximum is €100,000. The lead supplier may declare a lower final net price when submitting delivery.

€80,000

Caps: Alpha 50%, Bravo 30%, Charlie 25%, Delta 20%. Aggregate coverage is 125%.

Projected contributions

Why the total is exact: water-filling divides the price equally until a funder reaches its cap, then redistributes the remainder. Late pledges can lower everyone’s projected share until evidence closes pledging.

06 · Scope change

Change without moving the goalposts.

Not every discovery is an amendment. The decisive question is whether the protected promise changes for organizations that already pledged.

Clarification

The promise stays the same.

Explain ambiguity or implementation detail without changing outcome, criteria, maximum price, dates, targets, or supplier team.

Record visibly; no consent needed.
Material amendment

The protected terms change.

Publish an exact before/after version and rationale. Revalidate budgets and ask the fixed pledger group for explicit unanimous consent within the time box.

Apply only if everyone consents.
Additional outcome

The original promise still stands.

Do not smuggle optional work into the funded scope. Create a linked proposal or campaign with its own supplier terms and pledges.

Fund separately; preserve linkage.

08 · Scenario explorer

How the system behaves.

These representative flows cover the main design pressure points. The complete catalogue contains 67 detailed scenarios.

12 scenarios
Onboarding

A municipality joins

An employee signs in, registers or claims the organization, proves identity and account control, accepts terms, and receives delegated roles.

Budget

Innovation money is reserved

A budget manager creates a private envelope with amount, cost center, expiry, and optional component scope.

Demand

An idea becomes a proposal

A member submits privately; the relevant maintainer curates it into a public, endorsable need attached to canonical components.

Formation

Several suppliers form one team

One lead owns the platform workflow, co-suppliers confirm participation, and internal work and invoice division remain outside Bloei.me.

Funding

Pledges reach coverage

Caps reserve budget transactionally. Full coverage starts delivery automatically; the lead may deliberately start earlier and absorb the gap.

Funding

A late funder joins

Pledging stays open during delivery. A new cap can reduce every existing projected share until evidence submission fixes the group.

Change

Scope changes after pledging

One time-bounded amendment shows exact differences, reserves added exposure, and applies only after every affected pledger explicitly consents.

Review

A pledger objects

The full review stays open. A maintained criterion-based objection starts a fresh negative-consent vote; silence then counts as acceptance.

Conflict

A supplier cannot accept itself

A supplier organization has no acceptance voice on its own delivery. With no external pledgers, completion is explicitly self-funded.

Settlement

The lead closes the note

Bloei.me states what each pledger contributes, not to whom. The lead marks external settlement complete; pledgers do not mark payment.

Safety

AI flags risky wording

An optional assistant warns or prioritizes; temporary quarantine remains reversible and human-reviewed. It never governs campaigns.

Later

A municipality needs a contract

The trust-based pilot can later require a versioned external signed agreement before a pledge activates.
